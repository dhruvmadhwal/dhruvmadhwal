## Hi there 👋

I'm Dhruv Madhwal,a CS grad student at Arizona State University building reliable AI systems. I work on hallucination detection, multi-hop question answering, LLM evaluation, and compositional reasoning in vision-language models. I like taking AI/NLP ideas from research to working software through agents, retrieval systems, and ML applications. I’m equally excited by the systems behind them: event-driven architectures, distributed data platforms and scalable pipelines.

- 🔭 I’m currently working on advancing my thesis on hallucination detection and improving LLM reliability for multi-hop question answering.

## 🔬 Research

- **[Decomposed Prompting Does Not Fix Knowledge Gaps, But Helps Models Say “I Don’t Know”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04853)**  
  Accepted to ACL 2026; uses prompt-regime disagreement as a training-free signal for detecting hallucinations in closed-book multi-hop QA.

- **[Concrete Jungle: Towards Concreteness Paved Contrastive Negative Mining for Compositional Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13313)**  
  Studies how lexical concreteness improves hard negative mining and compositional reasoning in vision-language models.
